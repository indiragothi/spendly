Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you log: amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to run Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and breakdowns you see. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any other third party. If this changes, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at the email address provided on the Spendly website.
